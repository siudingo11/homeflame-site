Homeflame Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Homeflame ("we", "us") is an iPhone app that helps college students keep a streak of checking in with their parents. This policy explains what Homeflame collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Homeflame is published by Brady Stover.

The short version

  • Homeflame has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and never uses your location.
  • What you share in Homeflame (check-ins, notes, photos, videos with their sound, rewards and gifts) is visible only to the people in your family.
  • Homeflame does not move money. Rewards and gifts are notes about promises family members keep in real life. We do not collect bank, card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.

Who Homeflame is for

Homeflame is made for college students and their parents, and is for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Homeflame, contact us and we will delete the account.

What we collect

Your account. When you start or join a real family, Homeflame creates a private account for your phone automatically. It uses a random identifier and a random key stored on your device. We do not ask for your email address, phone number or a password.

Recovery code. So you can get back into your account on a new phone, the app shows you a recovery code. We store only a one-way hash of it (never the code itself), so we can check a code you enter but can't read yours. Making a new code replaces the old one, and using a code on a new phone signs the old phone out and replaces it.

Sign in with Apple (if you choose it, when available). If you link Sign in with Apple, we receive Apple's stable user identifier for this app so you can sign back in. We don't ask Apple for your name or email address.

Profile information you enter. Your display name (like "Mom" or "Avery"), your role in the family (parent or student), an optional school name, and an optional family name.

Family settings. The check-in schedule, which check-in types count, the daily goal, check-in moment hours, freezes, and streak records.

Check-ins. The check-in type (call, text, video chat, tap, selfie or vlog), the time, an optional note, whether it was on time, XP, and whether a parent confirmed or declined it, with an optional parent note. Logging a "call", "text" or "video chat" check-in is just a record you tap. Homeflame does not place calls, read your messages, or access your contacts or call history.

Photos, videos and audio (optional). If you add a selfie or a short vlog (up to 30 seconds) to a check-in, Homeflame uploads it to private cloud storage that only members of your family can view. Vlogs include the sound recorded by your microphone while you film; that audio is stored as part of the video. Homeflame uses the camera, microphone and photo library only when you choose to take or attach a photo or video. It never records audio in the background, and it doesn't collect audio any other way. (The app's own sound effects are played from the app and are not recordings.)

Reports. If you report a person, check-in, photo, video or gift message, we store the report: who filed it, the family, what was reported, the reason you picked, and any details you type. Reports are visible to the Homeflame team only, not to your family.

Rewards and gifts. Titles, notes, milestones, the type of reward (cash promise, gift card promise, experience, custom or note), an optional promised amount you type in, and whether it was opened, earned, given or redeemed. These are records of promises. No money or gift card moves through Homeflame.

Technical and security data. Our backend provider automatically processes basic request information, such as IP address, device and app version, and timestamps, to run the service, keep it secure, and prevent abuse. To limit repeated sign-ups and guesses of invite or recovery codes, we keep a one-way hash of the IP address (not the address itself) with the time of the attempt, and delete these records after about 2 days. We do not use this information to track you or to build advertising profiles.

On your device only. Sound preferences, which badges you have seen, a copy of your recovery code, and sample-family ("Try as parent / student") data stay on your phone and are not uploaded.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide Homeflame's features: syncing your family's check-ins, confirmations, streaks, rewards and gifts between your family's phones, showing your photos and videos to your family, letting you recover your account, reviewing reports, keeping the service secure, and responding to support requests. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see it

  • Your family. Members of your family (people who joined with your family's invite code) can see the family's check-ins, notes, photos, videos (with their sound), rewards and gifts. A mystery gift's contents stay hidden from the student until they open it. Anyone who has your invite code can join your family, so share it only with family members. A family is limited to 8 people. Parents can remove someone from the family and get a new invite code at any time in Settings.
  • The Homeflame team. Reports you file, and what they point to, so we can act on them.
  • Service providers. Homeflame's data is stored and processed by Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions) on servers in the United States. Apple provides the App Store, distributes the app, and runs Sign in with Apple if you use it. These providers process data only to run the service for us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or others.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and its data are deleted from our database and storage right away. Reports you filed stay so we can finish reviewing them, but they are no longer linked to your account. Hashed IP records for rate limits are deleted after about 2 days. Server logs kept by our infrastructure provider expire on their normal short schedule.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm.

  • Student: your account, check-ins, notes, photos, videos, and the gifts sent to you are permanently deleted. The family stays for everyone else.
  • Parent, with another parent in the family: your account and the gifts you sent are deleted. The family, its rewards and its invite code move to the other parent.
  • Parent, and you are the only parent: your account and the whole family are permanently deleted, including every check-in, photo, video, reward and gift. Students in the family stay signed in and can join a new family.

Signing out is different from deleting. If you lose your phone or delete the app, use your recovery code on the new phone (I already have an account on the welcome screen), then delete the account there if you want. If you don't have your recovery code, email us from the Support page and we will delete it for you (see Support for what to include).

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules keep each family's data separate, so a family member can read only their own family's records, and photos and videos are stored in a private bucket that only your family can access. Recovery codes are stored only as hashes, and repeated wrong invite or recovery codes are locked out for a while. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your display name and family settings in the app, make a new recovery code, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
