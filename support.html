Homeflame Support

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Need a hand with Homeflame? This page covers the common questions, how to reach us, and how to have your account deleted.

Contact us

Email: Siudingo223@gmail.com

Please include what happened, what you expected, and your iPhone and iOS version. Never send us passwords or bank or card details. Homeflame never asks for them.

Getting started

  • Parents: open Homeflame, tap I'm a parent, enter your name and create your family. You'll get a 6-letter invite code to send your student.
  • Students: open Homeflame, tap I'm a student with a code and enter the code your parent sent.
  • Just looking? Tap Try as parent or Try as student to explore a sample family that stays on your phone.
  • There's no email or password. Each phone gets its own private account automatically.
  • Save your recovery code. After you set up, the app shows a recovery code. Copy it somewhere safe. It's also in Settings → Account → Recovery code.
  • Homeflame is for college students and their parents, ages 13 and up.

Common questions

  • My check-in isn't counting yet. A check-in counts toward the streak once a parent confirms it. Ask them to open Homeflame and tap Confirm.
  • The invite code doesn't work. Codes are 6 letters or numbers and last 30 days. A parent can make a new one in Settings → Account → Get a new invite code. The old code stops working right away.
  • Someone joined our family who shouldn't have. A parent can remove them in Settings → Account → People in your family, then get a new invite code. Only the parent who created the family can remove another parent.
  • What is a freeze? A freeze covers one missed day automatically. Parents can gift up to 3.
  • New phone, or I reinstalled the app. On the welcome screen tap I already have an account and enter your recovery code. You'll be back in your family with your streak. The old phone is signed out and you get a new code.
  • Too many tries. After several wrong invite codes or recovery codes, the app makes you wait 15 minutes. That protects everyone's families from guessing.
  • Photos or videos won't upload. Check your connection, and make sure Homeflame has Camera, Microphone and Photos permission in the iPhone Settings app.

Reporting a problem

Something inappropriate, someone bothering you, or a stranger in your family? Tap Report on the check-in, photo or video, or gift message, or next to the person in Settings → Account → People in your family. Reports go to the Homeflame team, not your family. For anything urgent, email us at the address above. If someone is in danger, call 911.

Rewards and gifts are promises only

Rewards and gifts in Homeflame, including "cash promise" and "gift card promise", are notes about promises your family keeps in real life. Homeflame does not send or hold money, sell gift cards, or process payments. If a promise hasn't been kept, that's between you and your family. We can't pay out or refund anything, because no money moves through Homeflame.

Deleting your account

In the app (fastest): go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. Your account and its data are deleted right away. Here's what that means:

  • Student: your check-ins, photos, videos and the gifts sent to you are deleted. The family stays for everyone else.
  • Parent, with another parent in the family: your account is deleted and the family moves to the other parent.
  • Parent, and you're the only parent: the whole family is deleted, including every check-in, photo, video, reward and gift.

On a new phone: use your recovery code (I already have an account), then delete the account in Settings.

By email (if you don't have your recovery code): accounts have no email or password, so without the old phone or the recovery code the app can't get back in. Email us at the address above with the subject "Delete my Homeflame account" and include:

  • your display name and your family name as they appeared in Homeflame,
  • whether you were a parent or a student,
  • the family's invite code, if you still have it, or the name of another family member.

We'll match the account, confirm with you or another family member if needed, delete it with the same rules as in-app deletion, and reply when it's done (normally within 30 days).

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy. In short: no ads, no tracking, no location, and your family's check-ins, photos and gifts are visible only to your family.
